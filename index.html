This notice explains what this website collects, why, who can see it, and what you can ask me to do with it. It is here to be read — there is nothing to sign, and nothing on this page changes what you agreed to when you bought a package.

1. Who is behind this site

This site is run by Jude Michael Martinez, trading as Build with Judech, in the Philippines. One person handles it: there is no team and no third-party staff with access to your details. You can reach me through any channel in the Contact section.

2. What is collected, and when

Nothing is collected while you browse. Information is only recorded when you do one of these four things:

  • Sign in with Google. Your name, email address and profile picture, as Google gives them to me. I never see or receive your Google password.
  • Submit a payment. The name on the receipt, the payment method, the reference number, the amount, the date, the screenshot of your receipt, and the contact detail you choose to add.
  • Sign the package agreement. Your name, whether you are a student or a business, your school or organisation, your city or province, the date, your signature as you drew or typed it, and the browser you signed from.
  • Send a message. The title and the message, so I can answer it and so you can read the reply on your own account later.

The site also records when your account was last active, so I can tell whether you are waiting on a reply. There is no advertising, no analytics service, no tracking pixel and no third-party cookie anywhere on this site.

3. Why it is collected

Only to run the sale and the support that follows it: to check a payment is real, to unlock what you paid for on your account and no one else's, to keep a record of the agreement we both hold, and to answer you. Nothing is collected “in case it is useful later”.

4. Who can see it

Only me. Your details are not sold, rented, published, posted, shown to other buyers, or handed to anyone for marketing. I will not disclose them to a third party except in one case: where a law, a court or a government authority in the Philippines compels it, and only as far as it compels it.

Two suppliers necessarily hold the data in order to serve it: Supabase, which hosts the database and the file storage, and Google, which handles the sign-in. They are processors, not audiences — they hold the data to run the service, not to use it.

5. How it is kept

Receipts and signature images live in private storage: they are not on a public address, and even I open them through links that expire after an hour. Every record is fenced to the account that created it by database rules, not merely by what the page chooses to show — one buyer cannot read another buyer's payments, agreements or messages. Your purchases are also kept in your own browser so the page still knows what you own on that device; clearing your browser data clears that copy, not the record on the server.

No system is beyond reach, and I will not pretend otherwise. What I can promise is that nothing is exposed publicly by design, and that if a breach ever affected your details I would tell you and the National Privacy Commission, rather than keep quiet about it.

6. How long it is kept

Payment records and signed agreements are kept for as long as the sale needs to be provable — yours as much as mine — and for as long as Philippine law requires records of a transaction to be held. Messages are kept while the conversation is useful. Anything else you can ask me to delete at any time.

7. What you can ask for

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you may ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct anything wrong, delete what is no longer needed, or send you a copy. Ask through any contact channel and I will answer within a few days. Deleting an agreement or a payment record may mean the matching package can no longer be unlocked on your account — I will say so before doing it.

8. Children

If you are under 18, please have a parent, guardian or teacher involved before paying for anything here.

9. Changes

If this notice changes, the date below changes with it, and anything significant will be said in the reply to your next message rather than slipped in quietly.
